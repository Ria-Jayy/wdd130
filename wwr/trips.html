Half-Day Family Float

A relaxed, kid-friendly float through gentle rapids — perfect for first-timers, younger guests, or anyone who wants a scenic trip without the adrenaline. Runs 3 hours, including gear fitting and a riverside snack stop.

Full-Day Canyon Run

Our most popular trip. A full day of continuous whitewater through the canyon's biggest named rapids, with a hot riverside lunch halfway through. Recommended for guests 12 and up who want a real whitewater challenge.

Overnight Wilderness Expedition

Two days, one night, and miles of river with no roads in sight. Paddle by day, camp streamside by night, and fall asleep to the sound of the rapids. All camping gear and meals are included.
